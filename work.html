Operations Director

UM HackBeta

August 2025 - May 2026

On campus, I am a part of an organization called UM HackBeta. We plan the 24-hour annual coding event called a Hackathon. We work on planning throughout the fall and spring semesters, up until the date of the event in March. For the 2025-2026 year, I was operations director. This means I dealt with everything related to venue, schedule, activities during the event, and volunteers. Each director ran their own committee of volunteers, so mine was called the operations committee. It consisted of about 6 people, and I led this committee to complete all the tasks we needed to complete. Fun fact: my committee suggested creating a website for our event, and a sub-committee was created for website development. They created a website that was used for our event, something that had never been done before! I also got to work with venue managers, faculty members, and sponsors for the event. We ended the semester with a successful Hackathon with 50+ participants, 2+ sponsors, and many interesting submitted code projects. Overall, this entire experience taught me team collaboration, leadership, and committee management.
